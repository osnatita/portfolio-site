שירת הציוצים

Osnat Ita Skoblinski · Zuta, issue 115 · June 2011

Poems written on Twitter (within 140 characters)

*All the places have turned into airports with no departing flights. The stewardesses never sleep. The benches will never be comfortable. You can jump from the end of the jet bridges

*I love broken bridges that lead from a river to the same river and not from one bank to the other #Roma

*Allenby blues, he caught me by the light of the number sixteen, dozing, out of a shared taxi

*I want to throw my arms around the body of public diplomacy and have the arms of security hold me back

*On the roof the passion tree is collapsing onto the laundry lines. Some of the puddles are piss and some are a half-inflated pool. We projected an opera onto the solar panels

*With a bottle of wine we climbed the monument in Rabin Square. My flip-flop got stuck in the iron beams

*A mattress in the middle of the room. Dizengophia croaking through the shutters. Perfect sleep cycles dancing in a circle. This day does not begin at any particular point

Translator's notes

The body of public diplomacy / the arms of security. In Hebrew, גוף ההסברה and זרועות הביטחון are ordinary official terms, the hasbara apparatus and the security forces, but both are also body parts: a torso and a pair of arms. The poem takes them literally and asks to be held.

Dizengophia is דיזנגופיה, her coinage, from Dizengoff, the Tel Aviv street.

The passion tree is עץ התשוקה, the passionflower vine, which in Hebrew is literally the tree of desire.
